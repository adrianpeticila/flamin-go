## 1. What is a Flamin teardown?
An async roast of your first 5 seconds: hero, headline, CTA, proof. Friction points plus exact rewrites, in 24-48h. No call.

## 2. How fast do I get it?
24-48h async. You send the URL; you get a written teardown document. No kickoff, no debrief call.

## 3. What do you actually rewrite?
Headlines first, then subhead, CTA microcopy, proof order. You get before/after blocks pasted straight into the page, so your team ships without guessing. See the 49 USD Studio Pack.

## 4. Who is this for?
Solo founders and micro-SaaS teams of 1-5 people. Indie and bootstrapped. Allergic to enterprise process. If your launch needs a steering committee, this is not for you.

## 5. Do you touch design or only copy?
Copy drives it; design notes only where layout kills conversion (contrast, hierarchy, one primary CTA). No full redesign.

## 6. How is this different from a generic audit?
First-5-seconds lens, CTA friction scoring, paste-ready rewrites. Not a 40-page PDF with stock advice.

## 7. What do you need from me?
URL, one-line ICP, current CTA and price. Enough to start.

## 8. Do you guarantee conversion lift?
No. Teardowns raise clarity and click probability; traffic quality still matters. Shippable fixes guaranteed, not percentages.

## 9. What does the 49 USD Studio Pack include?
Written teardown plus rewrite blocks plus machine endpoints (llms.txt, ai-info.html). One-time, async, no subscription.

## 10. How do we start?
