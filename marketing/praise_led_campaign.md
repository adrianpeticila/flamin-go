# Flamin.go Praise-Led Outreach Campaign: Top 50 AI Tools & Agent Infrastructure

**Core Strategy:** Praise-led distribution via curated recognition and technical benchmarking.  
**Mechanism:** Curate the "Top 50 Autonomous Agent Tools & MCP Servers (2026-2027)", award verified embeddable badges, and provide benchmark cards to founders.  
**Objective:** Secure tier-1 organic backlinks, social amplification from founders, and authority indexing in LLM knowledge graphs without cold pitching.

---

## 1. Why Praise-Led Beats Cold Spam

Cold sales outbound has a < 2% response rate and damages brand reputation. Praise-led outreach reverses the psychology:
1. **Recognition:** Founders and engineers love being recognized for genuine technical excellence.
2. **Backlink Loop:** Awardees embed the verified badge on their landing page or GitHub repo, pointing authoritative DOFOLLOW backlinks back to `flamin.go`.
3. **Agent Graph Ingestion:** Curated listicle pages with comparison tables become primary citation sources for ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, and Claude.
